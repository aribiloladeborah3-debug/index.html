Project

GoFlex Academy — Free Webinar Promotion ("The Client Consistency System")

Objective

Increase webinar registrations by challenging the belief that more clients equals more income.

Audience
  • Freelancers with inconsistent monthly income
  • Dependent on Upwork / cold outreach
  • Stuck in the feast-or-famine cycle
Hidden Objection

"I've already tried getting more clients. Why would another program be different?"

HCAS Breakdown
  • Hook — "More Clients Won't Make You Rich," a contrarian line built to interrupt expectations.
  • Curiosity — opens a loop around why chasing clients keeps freelancers broke.
  • Agitation — the land-finish-search-repeat cycle, reinforced with a fisherman analogy.
  • Solution — the Client Consistency System, positioned as the alternative to chasing.

My Writing Framework

HCAS: most copy tries to sell immediately. Mine follows a different path.

People don't buy because they're convinced. They buy because they finally feel understood.

H

Hook

Open on the moment your reader is already living in.

C

Curiosity

Open a gap they need closed before they'll keep reading.

A

Agitation

Name the real cost of staying stuck — specific enough to sting a little.

S

Solution

Land the offer as relief. By now, they've asked for it.

How I Work

Three steps, all client-focused.

01

Understand your customer before writing

I study reviews, comments, and competitor ads to find the exact language your audience already uses.

02

Find the emotional gap

Why haven't they bought already? Fear, confusion, a bad past experience, a wrong belief — that's where strategy starts.

03

Turn insight into copy

Only after understanding your audience do I write emails, ads, or sales pages that sound written specifically for them.

Why My Copy Feels Different

Because I don't begin with writing. I begin with research.

Most copy starts with clever words. Mine starts with customer psychology.

Voice of Customer research

Real reviews and comments, not guesses — so the copy uses the words your audience already reaches for.

Customer awareness & market sophistication

How much your audience already knows, and how tired they are of hearing the same pitch, changes everything about how a piece should open.

Hidden objections

The real reason someone hesitates is rarely the one they'd say out loud. Finding it is where the strategy starts.

Angle & positioning

The same offer can be framed a dozen ways. I find the one your specific audience is already primed to hear.
